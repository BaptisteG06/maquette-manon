IV · The contract

Budgets slip when you sign a plan. Here, you sign a price.

Most bad surprises come from a vague quote and options discovered along the way. With Manon you sign a French house-building contract (CCMI). It fixes everything before work starts.

  1. 01
    One overall price

    Foundations, walls, roof, services, agreed finishes: everything is priced. Only the options you add change the total.

  2. 02
    A handover date

    In writing. If it slips, the contract provides penalties in your favour.

  3. 03
    A completion guarantee

    If the builder could not finish, a financial guarantor has the house completed at the agreed price.

  4. 04
    A protected deposit

    Refunded if planning permission is refused or the loan is not granted.

Frequently asked

Before you build.

Your question is not here? Keep it for the first meeting: it will get its answer.

Can the price go up during the build?

Not for anything written in the contract. The CCMI includes a guarantee of handover at the agreed price and date. Only options you add along the way change the price.

How many changes can I make to the plan?

As many as you need before signing. Each change is drawn on the model and priced on the spot: you decide with the plan and the price in front of you.

I don't have a plot yet. Is it too early?

It is often the best moment: a plot reads better once you know how you want to live on it. The office also offers plots in the area.

My plot is steep, narrow or hard to reach.

In the Alpes-Maritimes it is almost the rule. A slope can give levels, terraces and a view. We visit before drawing, to price the groundwork right.

How long does it take to build?

It depends on design, planning permission and the build. The handover date is written in the contract: you know it before you sign.

I live far away, or prefer English.

What matters is where the plot is. First conversations can happen remotely, and this site is also in English.
